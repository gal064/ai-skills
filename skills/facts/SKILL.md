What are the most important facts I should know about what we plan to build in this subject scope or next phase, or about what we completed if the work is done? Instead of an exhaustive list, include only the most critical facts and their implications for the existing system, future development, and end users. Assume I am new here but am a capable engineer. Avoid jargon, sloganese, and chiasmus. Be clear, direct, concise, and coherent.

<meta-rule>
This is a skill invocation. The user is asking for either what exactly is about to happen or what just happened. The user may invoke this either before or after work. This is not a holistic fact gathering exercise.

Subject scope means the work discussed, planned, or completed in the recent conversation.
</meta-rule>
